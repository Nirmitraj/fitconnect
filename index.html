THE IDEA BEHIND FITCONNECT

Project proposal

Assignment 1 · Front end

FitConnect is a social fitness website that brings personal activity, goals, and friends’ progress into one place. Users will be able to track workouts, understand activity trends, and celebrate achievements with their circle.

This first phase is a static, four-page prototype: Overview, My Dashboard, Friends, and Log a Workout. It uses semantic HTML5, an external responsive stylesheet, a workout history table, and a form with JavaScript validation. All fitness statistics and friend profiles are sample data.

A future database-backed phase will introduce user accounts, saved workout records, goals, friendships, and shared challenges. Privacy settings will let users choose what friends can see. The goal is to make consistent activity easier through clear progress tracking and social support.
